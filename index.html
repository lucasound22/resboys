FAQ

Straight answers

How do the sizes fit?

Hoodies are cut oversized, tees are a regular fit and bottoms are relaxed. Want it closer to the body? Size down. Between sizes or unsure? Email crew@resboys.com.au and we’ll sort you out.

How long does shipping take?

Standard is $10 (3–5 business days) and Express is $18 (1–2 business days). Free shipping on orders over $200 after discounts. We ship Australia-wide and email tracking when your order leaves.

Can I return or exchange something?

Yes — within 14 days of delivery for unworn items with tags on. Faulty or wrong item? Email us and we’ll fix it at no cost to you. Full details on the returns page.

How does the 15% off code work?

Join the crew and NEVERBLEND15 is applied to your cart automatically. It’s 15% off one order per customer and can’t be combined with other offers.

When’s the next drop?

Crew members hear first. Join the list and new drops land in your inbox before they hit the site.

Not yet — Australia only for now. Join the crew and you’ll hear first when that changes.

Where is my order?

You’ll get a confirmation straight after checkout and tracking once it ships. Anything else, email crew@resboys.com.au with your order number.
