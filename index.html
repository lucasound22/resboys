FAQ

Straight answers

How do the sizes fit?

Hoodies are cut oversized, tees are a regular fit and bottoms are relaxed. Want it closer to the body? Size down. Between sizes or unsure? Email crew@shop73.com.au and we’ll sort you out.

How long does shipping take?

Standard is $10 (3–5 business days) and Express is $18 (1–2 business days). Free shipping on orders over $200 after discounts. We ship Australia-wide and email tracking when your order leaves.

Can I return or exchange something?

Yes — within 14 days of delivery for unworn items with tags on. Faulty or wrong item? Email us and we’ll fix it at no cost to you. Full details on the returns page.

How does the 15% off code work?

Enter the circle and NEVERBLEND15 is applied to your cart automatically. It’s 15% off one order per customer and can’t be combined with other offers.

SIZE GUIDE

Hoodies — cut oversized. True size for the boxy look; size down for a closer fit.

T-shirts — regular fit. True to size.

Jackets & vests — cut for layering over a hoodie. Between sizes? Size up.

Bottoms — relaxed fit. True to size; between sizes, size down.

Snapbacks — one size.
