Privacy policy

Last updated 7 October 2026

73 ([add your legal / trading name], ABN [add your ABN]) respects your privacy and handles personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

  • Order details: name, email, phone, delivery and billing address, what you bought.
  • Email address if you join the crew list (with your consent).
  • Messages you send us.
  • If you accept analytics cookies: pages viewed, device and browser type, approximate location.

Payments

Card payments are processed by Stripe or Shopify Payments. Your card number is entered on their secure pages — we never see or store it.

How we use it

To fulfil and deliver orders, provide customer service, prevent fraud, meet legal and tax obligations, and — only if you opted in — send marketing emails. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email.

Who we share it with

Only the services needed to run the shop: payment processors, couriers, our email provider and (if you accept cookies) analytics providers. They may store data on servers outside Australia. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies

See our cookie policy.

Security and retention

We use reputable providers, HTTPS and access controls. We keep order records for as long as the law requires (generally 7 years for tax purposes) and delete marketing data when you unsubscribe or ask.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing crew@shop73.com.au. If you are not satisfied with our response you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
