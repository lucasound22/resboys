Size guide

Every 73 piece is cut for a streetwear fit. Use the notes below, and email us if you are unsure — we would rather help you pick than process a return.

PieceFitBetween sizes?
HoodiesOversized, boxySize down for a closer fit
T-shirtsRegularTrue to size
Jackets & vestsCut for layering over a hoodieSize up
Tracksuit jacketRegularSize up
Tracksuit pant, cargos, joggers, shortsRelaxedSize down
SnapbacksOne size, adjustable—

How to choose

Measure a piece you already like, lay it flat and compare. For hoodies, think about whether you want the boxy oversized look (your normal size) or a closer fit (one size down). Garment measurements for any piece are available on request: contact us.
